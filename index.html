Professional Biography

About Vikash Kumar

Bridging technology, structured client management, and turnkey interior execution.

Vikash Kumar is the Co-Founder of Elite Interior, an established interior design and turnkey execution firm headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. He directs the firm's digital strategy, web systems, client communication infrastructure, and operational growth.

Originally hailing from Bamarla, Serwa (Sedwa), in the Barmer district of Rajasthan, Vikash began his professional journey with a strong inclination toward business operations, technology, and organizational systems.

Prior to establishing Elite Interior, Vikash served as Marketing Manager at Ramdev Interior Pvt. Ltd., where he acquired valuable on-ground exposure to client acquisition, market coordination, vendor dynamics, and the complexities of the interior execution industry in Gujarat.

Recognizing the opportunity to bring greater transparency, modern project tracking, and digital efficiency to interior solutions, he co-founded Elite Interior alongside Ramesh Taak. While Ramesh leads the physical on-ground site execution and technical craftsmanship with over 12 years of hands-on experience, Vikash leads the technology, digital marketing, client onboarding pipelines, and operational architecture.

Under their shared leadership, Elite Interior delivers full home interiors, modular kitchens, and custom residential spaces across Ahmedabad from their office at Sun South Trade, South Bopal.
